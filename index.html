Byblos Tower

Explore a 3D reconstruction inspired by the historic city of Byblos, Lebanon. Rotate the model with your finger, then tap View in AR on a compatible phone to place it in your surroundings.

On iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap “View in AR”, and move the camera slowly to detect a flat surface.
Byblos · Jbeil · جبيل
Byblos is one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities, located on Lebanon's Mediterranean coast. This interactive model invites you to explore a piece of its architectural heritage.
